Smart Dashboard Cards
In the blue cards, entrepreneurs have a quick overview of their company’s financial status — how much they earned that month and whether there are any unpaid charges. Based on how they use the app, they may also see cards with personalized suggestions or product offers like loans and credit cards.

Start a sale
This section works like a POS device and allows the business owner to quickly initiate a sale. Under Pix, they can receive payments via a key or by displaying a QR Code. In Payment Link, they generate a link that can be shared through social media. With Tap, the phone itself becomes a POS terminal and can receive payments via NFC.

Latest Sales
This area shows the latest transactions from the My Business account, making it easier to check a received payment.
